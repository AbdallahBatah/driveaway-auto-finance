How auto financing works here

The plain version

Auto financing sounds complicated until someone explains it in normal words. You borrow money to buy the car, you pay it back in monthly payments, and the car secures the loan. Our job is to find a payment that fits your budget and a term you can live with.

What we look at

Lenders care about three things: your income, your down payment and the car you choose. Credit history matters, but it is not the only line on the page. People with thin credit or a rough patch behind them still come to us for auto financing, and some of them drive home the same day.

What to bring

A recent pay stub or other proof of income, proof of where you live such as a utility bill, a valid ID and your down payment. Reviewers often mention $500 down, though your amount may be higher or lower depending on the car. One reviewer says we found a way even without a driver’s license at the time. Ask us what your options are.

Keeping the payment sane

Try the calculator with a longer term and a shorter one. A longer term lowers the payment but costs more interest in the end. Put down as much as you comfortably can, because every dollar down is a dollar you do not pay interest on. Good auto financing should leave you room to buy groceries. We would rather see you in a modest car with a payment you can handle than a nicer one you will struggle with.

Questions people ask

Can I get auto financing with bad credit? Often yes, with the right car and a down payment. Can my trade-in count as the down payment? It can. Does the quick form on this page pull my credit? No. It asks for a callback, and the full credit application is completed with our team, so ask how your credit will be checked before you sign.

Sell your car without the runaround

Two honest options

If you want to sell your car, you can sell it to a dealer or to a stranger from the internet. We are the first kind. We buy cars at Driveaway Auto Finance and we want the process to be short.

What affects the offer

Year, make, model, mileage and condition set the number, along with what similar cars are going for right now. A clean car with service records usually gets a better offer than one with warning lights and a dented door. Tell us about problems up front. It saves everybody time.

Selling versus trading in

When you sell your car outright, you get paid and walk away. When you trade it in, its value goes toward the next car, which can cover your down payment and shrink what you need to finance. Not sure which is better? Ask for both numbers.

Still owe money on it?

That is common. Tell us who holds the loan and roughly what you owe. If the car is worth more than the payoff, the difference is yours. If it is worth less, we will go through the options with you so there are no surprises later.

What to bring

Your ID, the title if you have it, the registration, the keys and the payoff details if there is a loan. Ready to sell your car? Fill in the form above with the VIN and mileage, or call 219-945-3136 and we will start there.

Why sell your car to a local lot

Nobody shows up at your driveway, nobody asks to take it on a long test drive alone, and nobody wants a deposit first. You bring the car to 2333 W 37th Ave in Hobart and deal with people you can look in the eye. Then, if you need another vehicle, look through our inventory while you are here.

How the process goes

You bring the car, we look it over and we talk numbers. The look is usually a walk around, a peek under the hood, a short drive and a check of the paperwork. Then you get an offer, in cash or as trade-in credit. You are free to say no. If you say yes, we take care of the next steps with you.

Mistakes that cost sellers money

Cleaning the car costs little and helps. Hiding a problem usually backfires, because we will find it. Waiting too long can cost you as well, since a car loses value every month. And selling without knowing your loan payoff leads to unpleasant surprises, so call your lender first.

Questions about selling

Does the car have to be perfect? No. Mileage and wear are expected on used vehicles, and the offer reflects them. Do I need an appointment? Calling first is smart, but you can stop by too. Can I bring someone with me? Of course. A second opinion never hurts when money is involved.

What our customer reviews have in common

The same few themes

We have 163 customer reviews across our review sites, and when you read enough of them, the same things come up again and again. The words people use are fast, friendly and a $500 down payment.

Speed

Several reviewers say they were finished in a couple of hours. One says she applied one day and drove off the next. Fast is not a promise for every deal, but it is how many of our customer reviews describe the experience.

Down payments

The number $500 shows up in a lot of these stories. People mention coming in with $500 down and leaving in a Chevy Blazer or the car they wanted. Your situation and the car decide your actual number, so treat $500 as a pattern, not a guarantee.

Credit

A few customer reviews mention credit that was not great, or no driver’s license, and still getting approved. We do not say yes to everybody and we do not publish anyone’s file, but we do work hard to find a way.

Where these come from

These are highlights taken from the review sites where customers wrote them. The full list and the star rating are on our Google page. If something went wrong for you, call 219-945-3136 and ask for the manager. We would rather hear it from you directly and fix it.

Leave your own

Bought from us? A review on Google helps the next person decide. Be specific about what happened, because that is what makes customer reviews useful. Thinking about buying? Start with the inventory or the financing page.

What reviews cannot tell you

A review is one person’s afternoon. It cannot tell you what your approval will look like or whether a certain car is right for you. Use customer reviews to learn how we treat people, then judge the cars and the numbers for yourself.

Details worth noticing

People talk about being treated kindly, about patience when they were nervous about credit and about getting through the paperwork quickly. Several say the staff explained each step. Others like that the cars looked good and drove well. Those are small things, but they decide whether you would recommend a dealership to a friend.

Before you decide

Read the newest reviews first, since they show how we work today. Look for details instead of just stars. And visit the lot, because customer reviews are only half of it. The other half is how you feel when you walk in.
